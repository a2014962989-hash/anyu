All remaining exact representatives of the accepted Chinese release inventory were included after exclusion of the entire 55 components involved in the previous pilot. Components are kept together and purposes were assigned before new human labels or predictions. Source keys are preserved as release metadata; string components do not establish user, event or platform independence.

Primary labels distinguish suspicious drug commerce, noncommercial content, insufficient information and unusable text. Positive judgments require object and commerce evidence; the original two annotations are retained and disagreements go to a third human. Confirmation answers are to be sealed by the coordinator until the final method, controls, checkpoints and analysis are frozen.

Seven workbooks were created: six blinded annotation workbooks and one coordinator workbook. All 4,323 IDs, texts and batch allocations were checked on workbook roundtrip; annotation fields are blank. Instruction and table layouts were visually inspected. No human annotation, new fit or confirmation result is claimed.
